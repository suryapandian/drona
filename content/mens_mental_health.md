## It's okay to not be okay

Men are often told to "be strong", "adjust", and "not cry like a girl". Many of us grow up believing that asking for help is a sign of weakness. It isn't. Carrying the pressure of work, money, family and expectations all alone is what wears us down.

This November, for **Men's Mental Health Month**, we are offering **6 free therapy sessions** to men who would like to talk to a professional. No judgement, no lectures, just a space to be heard.

## Who can register
- Any man aged 18 or above
- You don't need to be "in crisis" to talk to someone. Stress, low mood, relationship troubles, or simply feeling stuck are all good reasons.

## How it works
1. Fill out the form below. It takes about 5 minutes.
2. We will reach out to you on phone or WhatsApp to match you with a therapist.
3. You get 6 sessions, free of cost.
